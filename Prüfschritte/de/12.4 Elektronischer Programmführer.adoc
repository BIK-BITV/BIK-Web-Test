[.BLOCK_LANG_EN]
==== 12.4 Electronic program guides

____
[.BLOCK_LANG_EN]
--
Where ICT is an Electronic Programme Guide (EPG),
the EPG shall provide information on the availability of subtitles, audio description, spoken subtitles, and sign language
interpretation of the media.

NOTE: 
Best practice is to include information about all accessibility features as they are developed and standard
formats for including information are developed.
--
