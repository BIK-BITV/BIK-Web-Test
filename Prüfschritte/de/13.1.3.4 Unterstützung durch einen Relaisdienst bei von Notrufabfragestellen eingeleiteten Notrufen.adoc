[.BLOCK_LANG_EN]
==== 13.1.3.4 Relay service support during emergency communications initiated by the PSAP
____
[.BLOCK_LANG_EN]
--
Where ICT is, or includes, a relay service,
the relay service shall accept invocations by emergency communication PSAPs.

NOTE: 
The relay service is expected to allow invocations by PSAPs in a three-party fashion and perform the
relaying action in the ways specified in ETSI EN 303 919 [i.56], clauses 9 and 10.
--
