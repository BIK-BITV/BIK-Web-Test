[.BLOCK_LANG_EN]
==== 13.1.2.6 Relay service support requested by the primary user for emergency communications
____
[.BLOCK_LANG_EN]
--
Where ICT is intended to support relay service inclusion in communication, and is, or includes, relay service invocation
functions,
the ICT shall initiate and perform emergency communications on the request by the primary user and invoke relay
service.

NOTE: 
The relay service invocation functions are expected to allow invocations of PSAPs in a three-party
fashion and perform the relaying action in the ways specified in ETSI ES 202 975 [i.6], clauses 6.5.1.
--
____
