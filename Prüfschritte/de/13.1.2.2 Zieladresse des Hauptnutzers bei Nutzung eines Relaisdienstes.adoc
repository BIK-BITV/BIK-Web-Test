[.BLOCK_LANG_EN]
==== 13.1.2.2 Use of the primary user destination address in relayed communications
____
[.BLOCK_LANG_EN]
--
Where ICT is intended to support relay service inclusion in communication, and is, or includes, relay service invocation
functions,
the relay service invocation functions shall use destination addresses given to the primary users by their communication
systems.

NOTE: 
For more details see clause 6.3 in ETSI ES 202 975 [i.6].
--
____
